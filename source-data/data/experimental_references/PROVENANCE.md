# Thermal-expansion experimental reference

The experimental coefficients are transcribed from Peipei Gao,
*Anelastic Relaxation and Phase Transformations in Doped Binary Oxides*, PhD
dissertation, Texas A&M University, August 2016, Table 2.1, printed page 32
(PDF page 48).

Public primary source:
https://oaktrust.library.tamu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/0089cc80-f7d9-4d0d-8df1-700be5f05fb3/content

Table 2.1 reports XRD coefficients over 30–800 °C and mean TMA coefficients
over 30–900 °C. For 8YSZ/10YSZ, the XRD values are 10.51/10.56 and the TMA
values are 10.31/10.38, in 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹. The dissertation describes straight-line
thermal-strain fits on printed page 35 (PDF page 51), and instantaneous
coefficients on printed page 37 (PDF page 53). The samples have approximately
5 µm grains.

Related journal publication: Gao, P., Bolon, A., Taneja, M., Xie, Z., Orlovskaya, N. & Radovic, M. *Thermal expansion and elastic moduli of electrolyte materials for high and intermediate temperature solid oxide fuel cell*. Solid State Ionics300,1–9(2017). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssi.2016.11.015

The dissertation table is the direct numerical source. The related journal
publication identifies the corresponding experimental study. Both sources
are cited with their respective roles.

The DICE log-length fits over 303.15–1173.15 K give 11.72 ± 0.14 and
11.78 ± 0.08 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹ for 8YSZ and 10YSZ. The comparison uses the TMA
column for the matching temperature interval. Simulation fits use log length;
the experimental fits use reference-normalised thermal strain.

`verified_experimental_coefficients.csv` contains the transcribed coefficients,
temperature intervals and source identifiers.
